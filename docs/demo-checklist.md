# 5-Minute Live Viva Demo Checklist — CloudQuant

Follow this sequence during your evaluation:

| Step | Action | What to Explain to the Examiner |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Open Dashboard** | Point out the disclaimer banner clearly stating that this is an educational paper-trading simulation. Highlight initial virtual capital (₹1,00,000) and 0 starting trades. |
| **2** | **Select Equity & View Charts** | Select `RELIANCE.NS` or `TCS.NS`. Show the Price chart with the 10-day Fast SMA and 30-day Slow SMA crossover lines, followed by the 14-day RSI oscillator. |
| **3** | **Explain the Strategy Signal** | Click on the **Strategy Signals & Execution** tab. Show the current computed signal (BUY, SELL, or HOLD) and read the natural language **Explainability & Rationale** generated by the engine. |
| **4** | **Execute Algorithmic Trade** | Click `Run Strategy`. Show the notification confirming execution. Click it a second time to demonstrate the **Anti-Duplicate Risk Guard** preventing duplicate order spam. |
| **5** | **Demonstrate Risk Controls (Manual Order)** | Go to the **Manual Paper Trading** tab: <br/>1. Attempt to buy ₹5,00,000 worth of stock — show the red **"Insufficient funds"** error.<br/>2. Attempt to sell 100 shares of a stock not owned — show the red **"Oversell rejected"** error. |
| **6** | **Execute a Valid Order** | Execute a valid BUY order for 10 shares. Show the **Holdings Table** update instantly with average buy price and unrealized P&L. |
| **7** | **Demonstrate Offline Resiliency** | Toggle **"Force Sample-Data Mode"** in the sidebar. Show that charts, indicators, and strategies continue to function seamlessly even with zero external internet connectivity. |
| **8** | **Audit Trail & Cloud Architecture** | Scroll to the **Recent Order History** audit ledger. Show timestamps and FILLED/REJECTED statuses. Conclude by explaining the $0.00 AWS Free Tier architecture. |
